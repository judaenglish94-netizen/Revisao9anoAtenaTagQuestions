Guia Definitivo: Tag Questions

Resumo gramatical completo, regras por tempo verbal e exceções

💡 Regra de Ouro: Se a oração principal for afirmativa, a tag será negativa. Se a oração for negativa, a tag será afirmativa. A tag SEMPRE usa o verbo auxiliar/modal correspondente + pronome reto!

1. Estrutura Básica

Tag Questions são pequenas perguntas no final de uma frase usadas para confirmar uma informação.

• Frase (+), Tag (-): You speak English, don't you?

• Frase (-), Tag (+): You don't speak French, do you?

Nota: A tag negativa usa sempre a forma contraída (don't, isn't, haven't, etc.).

2. Aplicação em Todos os Tempos Verbais

A) Simple Present (Auxiliares: do / does / to be)

• She lives in London, doesn't she?

• They don't play chess, do they?

• He is a teacher, isn't he?

B) Present Continuous (Auxiliares: am / is / are)

• You are studying now, aren't you?

• She isn't sleeping, is she?

C) Simple Past (Auxiliares: did / was / were)

• You traveled last week, didn't you?

• He didn't call you, did he?

• They were happy, weren't they?

D) Past Continuous (Auxiliares: was / were)

• It was raining yesterday, wasn't it?

• We weren't sleeping at 10, were we?

E) Simple Future (Will & Be Going To)

• They will arrive tomorrow, won't they?

• She isn't going to buy that house, is she?

F) Present Perfect (Auxiliares: have / has)

• You have finished the test, haven't you?

• He hasn't seen this movie, has he?

G) Past Perfect (Auxiliar: had)

• The train had already left, hadn't it?

• They hadn't eaten before, had they?

H) Verbos Modais (can, could, should, must)

• She can drive a truck, can't she?

• You shouldn't stay up late, should you?

3. Exceções Importantes (Atenção Máxima!)

🚨 1. Primeira pessoa "I am":

• I am right, aren't I? (Não existe "amn't I" em inglês padrão).

• Porém na negativa: I am not late, am I?

🚨 2. Convite com "Let's":

• Let's go to the beach, shall we?

🚨 3. Imperativos (Ordens/Pedidos):

• Close the door, will you? / won't you?

• Don't make noise, will you?

🚨 4. Palavras com sentido negativo (never, nobody, nothing, seldom, hardly):

A frase já é considerada negativa, portanto a tag será AFIRMATIVA!

• You never eat meat, do you?

• Nothing happened, did it?

🚨 5. Substituição de Sujeitos por Pronomes:

• Nobody / Somebody / Everyone / Everybody $\rightarrow$ Pronome THEY na tag.

Ex: Everyone knows him, don't they?

• Nothing / Something / Everything / This / That $\rightarrow$ Pronome IT na tag.

Ex: This is your book, isn't it?
